The LP Compressor Rotor Trim Balance Procedure Calculated From In-flight or Ground Run Vibration Data (Single Shot Fan Trim Balance).
Reference copy: TPA customization · Revision 5 · 01-Jul-2026.

Calculation scope

Ground: three readings. Flight: one reading. N1: tabulated values from 76 to 88%, without interpolation. Cumulative A/B combinations are selected with a preference for undercorrection. With existing bolts, VE + VD = VT is calculated by summing each position, per figure 71-00-00-991-18300-22-A, detail 2. Alternative non-cumulative combinations in section 4.C require separate evaluation. Final selection is limited to the implemented cumulative A/B rows; no layout is issued if the target is outside that range. The app does not verify mechanical conditions, installation or subsequent engine acceptance.
